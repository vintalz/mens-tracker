When to seek medical review

Consider medical evaluation if bleeding lasts >7 days, cycles are repeatedly <21 or >35 days apart, cycle length varies substantially, you have bleeding between periods/after sex, or bleeding is unusually heavy. Seek urgent care for very heavy bleeding with dizziness, fainting, weakness, shortness of breath or a racing pulse.
